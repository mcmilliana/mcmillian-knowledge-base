\# Journaling



* Journaling helps support mental health by helping you process your emotions, reduce stress, and gain clarity on your thoughts and daily patterns. Data shows it can serve as a low-risk tool to ease anxiety, support mood regulation, and complement professional mental health. It helps by building self awareness, spots patterns, and reduces overwhelming stress. Journaling is often paird with \[\[spirituality]] as people believe that they can connect with their higher power through writing as some feel that God is the all seeing and hearing.



* Journaling can pair well with counseling, but it does not replace professional care for ongoing distress, trauma, or clinical mental health conditions. If you need immediate assistance call 988 or 911 for emergencies.
