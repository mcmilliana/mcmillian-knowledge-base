# Physical Fitness

* Physical fitness is great for mental health crisis as it acts as a natural defense method against stress, anxiety, and depression by altering both brain chemistry and a person's emotional resilience. The use of physical movement stimulates the release of endorphins, serotonin, and dopamine—neurotransmitters that promotes a positive mood, sharpens focus, and promotes calming, while simultaneously reducing excess cortisol (which leads to obesity) and adrenaline caused by chronic stress. Over the course of time, regular movement also supports neurogenesis, improves memory, sleep quality, and cognitive flexibility.




* Beyond the biological benefits gained from exercise, maintaining a fitness routine builds long lasting psychological strength. By setting and achieving physical goals, a person fosters self-efficacy, confidence, and a tangible sense of control over one's body and daily habits. Group workouts or outdoor activities are highly encouraged because they combat isolation by creating a social connection and routine. Whether through brisk walking, strength training, or yoga, consistent physical activity serves as a reliable, accessible coping tool that stabilizes mood, discharges pent-up nerves, and strengthens overall mental well-being. The use of [[breathing techniques]] while performing physical fitness, helps relieve anxiety and resets the frontal cortex as you are doing a mental reset because breathing is very important while performing physical exercises.
